Delete your account

You can delete your Ilmiva account, by NayaByte Studio, at any time. Your account and learning data are removed within 30 days of your request.

If you still have the app

  1. Open Ilmiva and sign in.
  2. Open the Profile tab.
  3. Choose Delete account, confirm, and tap Request deletion.

If you no longer have the app

  1. Write an email from the address you use to sign in to Ilmiva. We can only accept requests from that address, so nobody else can delete your account.
  2. Send it to nayabytestudio@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Ilmiva account".
  3. We reply to confirm, then complete the deletion within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, roll number, batch and phone number.
  • Courses you unlocked and the access codes you redeemed.
  • Lesson progress, notes and practice quiz answers.
  • Registered phone records and usage events.

What is kept

Only a record that the deletion was completed, with your email address partly hidden. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for a short time until those backups are replaced.
