Privacy policy

Ilmiva is a course library for students. We collect only what it takes to sign you in, unlock your courses, keep your place, and stop material from being shared without permission.

Effective 23 September 2026. Published by NayaByte Studio.

What we collect

When you create an account

  • Your email address, used to send one-time sign-in codes.
  • Your name, and the roll number and batch or class you enter when you enrol.
  • A phone number, only if you choose to add one to your profile.

From your phone

  • A scrambled device identifier. The app reads your phone's Android ID, combines it with your account and a secret value, and sends only the scrambled result. The real Android ID never leaves your phone, and the result cannot be matched across different accounts.
  • Your phone's model name, Android version and the Ilmiva app version.

While you study

  • Which access codes you redeemed and the courses they unlocked.
  • Lesson progress, notes you write, and practice quiz answers.
  • Simple usage events, such as opening a chapter, so your instructor can see whether material is being read.

For security

  • Failed access-code attempts, so repeated guessing can be stopped.
  • Your IP address and basic browser signals, processed by our hosting and anti-abuse providers when you sign in.

We do not collect payment details, contacts, location, photos, microphone input or the advertising ID. Ilmiva shows no ads and contains no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account on one registered phone.
  • To show only the courses your access code unlocks.
  • To save your progress so you can continue where you stopped.
  • To mark lesson pages with a faint watermark showing your name, a partly hidden email address and the date. This discourages sharing and helps trace leaked material.
  • To give your instructor course statistics, such as how many students opened a chapter.
  • To prevent abuse, such as fake sign-ups and code guessing.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it

These companies process data on our behalf, only to run Ilmiva:

Supabase
Stores the database and handles sign-in. Servers are in Mumbai, India. Data is encrypted in transit.
Google (Gmail)
Sends the email containing your sign-in code.
Cloudflare Turnstile
Runs a short security check before a sign-in code is sent, to block automated abuse.
GitHub Pages
Hosts this page and the account-deletion page.

Your instructor, as the course organiser, can see your name, email, roll number, batch, courses, progress and last activity.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists, including after a course or batch ends, so your history stays available if you return. Security records such as failed code attempts are kept only as long as they are useful for preventing abuse. When you delete your account, everything linked to you is removed within 30 days. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for a short time until those backups are replaced.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Profile and choose Delete account. If you no longer have the app, follow the steps on the account deletion page. We delete your profile, course access, progress, notes, quiz answers, device records and usage events. We keep a record that a deletion was completed, with your email address partly hidden.

Your choices

  • You can view and correct your name, roll number, batch and phone number in Profile.
  • You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you by emailing us from the address on your account.
  • You can delete your account at any time.

Ilmiva is meant for students aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.
